Problems With Recurrent Neural Networks
    1) Problem of long term dependency
    2) Stragnated training

Example : i went to delhi. i love the food. i love the places.
            Among all of them , i love the RedFort

1) problem of long term dependency
RNN works well with short sequences but fails capturing long term sequences.
    rnn fails to understand where RedFort belongs to..

2) Stagnated training(learning stops while backpropagating through time)
RNN works well with long term data but fails capturing the earlier information

while updating wi and wh weights , we have to calculate the gradients in backpropagation

gradient formula : dowL/dowy * dow y/dow h3 * dow h3/dow w + dowL/dowy * dow y/dow h3 * dow h3/ dow h2 * dow h2/ dow w
    +.......

    so gradient multiplication increases for earlier information when backpropagating from end
    we use tanh mean -1 to 1 so when gradients increases , multiplying less than 1 values 
    multiple times makes particular information value become very small or erase

    so the recent or ending information carried by RNN but earlier starting information is erased.

    this is called vanishing gradient problem

    so i love red fort information stays but i went to delhi is erased

possible solutions to overcome VGP:
    1) ReLU
    2) LSTM

when using ReLU , max(0, x)
    values becomes very high when no of terms or gradients increases
    usually starting terms has more gradients so the value increases more
    and recent or end information becomes very less or erased.

    this problem is known as Exploding gradient problem

    possible solutions are :
        1) Gradient clipping (use threshold to stop gradients becoming very large)
        2) Use LSTM's
